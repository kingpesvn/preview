    Good to know

    How do I know it’s genuine?

    We buy from official distributors and keep an invoice for every batch. Ask to see it when you shop in store.

    Will this cure my acne?

    Cosmetics only support skin care. For inflamed, widespread or lasting acne, see a dermatologist.

    Can I return an opened product?

    Opened products can’t be returned for hygiene reasons. Ask for a sample before buying full size.

    How long does delivery take?
