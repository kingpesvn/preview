Good to know

How much does property management cost?

Most owners pay 8–12% of the monthly rent, plus a leasing fee when a new tenant moves in. Use the fee calculator above to see your yearly cost and what you keep.

How do you screen tenants?

We check income, rental history and references, credit and background, using the same written criteria for every applicant.

What counts as an emergency repair?

Water you cannot stop, no heat in freezing weather, sewage backup, no power or a lockout. Call the emergency line any time; for everything else, send a request online.

Can I keep my current tenant?

Yes. We take over the lease as it is, inspect the home and start collecting rent the next month.

Are pets allowed?

In homes marked "cats", "dogs" or "pets OK", with a pet deposit and a small monthly pet fee. Service and support animals are always welcome.
