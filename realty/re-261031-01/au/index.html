Documents

What do you need?

A starting list; some lenders ask for more.

Salaried

  • Photo ID for each borrower
  • Marriage certificate if applicable
  • Employment contract or letter
  • Payslips for the last 3–6 months

Self-employed

  • Business registration
  • Bank statements for 6–12 months
  • Tax returns or financial statements
  • Lease for business premises if any

The property

  • Contract of sale or deposit receipt
  • Title details from the seller
  • Details of any other security offered

Good to know

Do you charge for advice?

No. Lenders pay us a commission when your loan settles; you pay nothing extra.

How is the rate worked out after the fixed period?

Usually the lender’s reference rate plus a fixed margin set in your contract. Ask about the margin and how the reference rate is published before you sign.

Can I borrow if I am paid in cash?

Possibly, if you can show steady income through records, contracts or bank statements. Each lender has its own policy.

How much of the price can I borrow?

Usually around 60–80% of the valued price, depending on the property and lender. The valuation can be lower than your purchase price.

How long does it take?

Usually 1–3 weeks from a complete application. We update you at each step.
