    Good to know

    How far ahead should we book?

    Most couples book 10 to 16 months ahead. Saturdays from May to October go first; Fridays and Sundays are often still open.

    Do you travel to Italy?

    Yes, every summer. The travel fee covers flights and two nights; there is no fee for the extra day we spend scouting the venue.

    How many photos will we get?

    Around 80 to 100 per hour of coverage, all edited by hand. No filters, no watermarks.

    Is the retainer refundable?

    The 30% retainer holds your date and is not refundable, but you can move it once to another free date within 18 months.
