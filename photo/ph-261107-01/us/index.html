    Will this lens fit my camera?

    Open Build a kit and choose your body; only lenses with the same mount appear. Already own a camera? Message us its model and we will check.

    Do you offer instalments?

    Yes, via many banks’ credit cards; terms and fees depend on the bank and we confirm them with your order.

    How are pre-owned cameras covered?

    Pre-owned bodies get a 30-point check, a listed shutter count and a 3-month store warranty.

    Can I see the camera before paying?

    Yes. Choose in-store pickup and we hold the items for 3 days so you can try before paying.
