    Good to know

    When can I order?

    Orders open about 50 days before the Mid-Autumn full moon and we deliver until the day before. Out of season you can pre-order for next year.

    How long do the cakes keep?

    Baked cakes about 30 days, sticky rice about 10, snow skin 5–7 days in the fridge. Check the date printed on each cake.

    Do you have vegan or low-sugar cakes?

    Yes: vegan cakes (egg-free crust, no animal fat) and reduced-sugar cakes; use the "Vegan" or "Low sugar" filter. Low sugar still contains sugar, so people with diabetes should ask their doctor.

    How do company orders work?

    Enter the number of boxes on a product page: 5% off from 20, 10% from 50, 15% from 100. Logo printing from 50 boxes; we send a proof first and take a list of delivery addresses.

    Do you ship abroad?

    We deliver within Vietnam only. From abroad, you can order for family in Vietnam and pay by card with a link we send.
