      Good to know

      Do you take reservations?

      No, it is walk-in at the counter. Check the usual wait above, or order ahead for pickup to skip the line.

      Is there a vegetarian or vegan ramen?

      The vegan tantan has no animal products. Edamame, rice and drinks are vegetarian too. Gyoza and karaage are not.

      Can I choose noodle firmness?

      Yes: soft, regular, firm or extra firm. Pick it in the order form; for dine-in, tell the counter.

      How long does pickup ramen stay good?

      Eat within 30 minutes for the best noodles. Broth and noodles are packed apart; heat the broth and pour it over.

      Do you have gluten-free noodles?

      Not yet. Our noodles are wheat, and the kitchen shares fryers, so we cannot promise gluten-free.
