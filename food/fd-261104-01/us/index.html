Good to know

Do I need to book?

Friday to Sunday evenings, yes. Groups of 15+ please book a day ahead.

Is there something not spicy?

Yes. Mushroom broth, beef shabu and most grilled dishes are mild; the split pot has a mild side.

How does hot pot delivery work?

We deliver the pot, broth, ingredients and a mini stove within 5 km and collect the stove next day.

Do you have a private room?

Yes, for 20–30 people, with a projector for birthdays and office parties.
