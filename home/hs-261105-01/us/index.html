Good to know

Is the page price final?

No, it’s an estimate. After the survey the price goes into the contract and doesn’t change if your belongings match the survey.

What if trucks can’t reach my street?

We shuttle with a small van or carry to the street; it’s included in the survey quote.

How far ahead should I book?

Ideally 3–5 days ahead, earlier for month-ends and weekends. Same-day moves are possible if a truck is free.

What if something gets damaged?

We check with you when unloading; transport damage is compensated as written in the contract.
