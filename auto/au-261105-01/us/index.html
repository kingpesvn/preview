Good to know

How do I find my tyre size?

Look for the code on your tyre sidewall (e.g. 205/55R16) or the sticker in the driver’s door frame. Send us a photo if unsure.

How long do tyres last?

It depends on driving and roads. Replace when the tread reaches the wear bars or the tyre shows cracks or bulges. We check for free.

Should I replace all 4 tyres at once?

At least in pairs on the same axle. Replacing all 4 keeps grip even and fitting is free.

Will these alloy wheels fit my car?

Diameter, bolt count, PCD and offset must match. Send your model and year and we’ll check before you order.
