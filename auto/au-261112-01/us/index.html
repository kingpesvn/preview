Good to know

What if my flight is delayed?

Enter your flight number when booking and the driver follows the real landing time. No extra charge for delays.

Where do I meet the driver?

Your driver stands at the arrivals exit after baggage claim with a name sign. You get the driver’s number before pickup.

How do I pay?

Pay the driver in cash or by bank transfer on arrival. Invoices are available for companies.

Is there a night surcharge?

Pickups from 10 pm to 5 am add 20%. The form adds it automatically when you pick the time.
