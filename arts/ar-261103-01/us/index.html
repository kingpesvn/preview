    Good to know

    Which instrument is best for a beginner?

    Ukulele is the easiest start; a nylon-string classical guitar suits kids; piano suits those who want solid music theory.

    Can I try an instrument I order online?

    Yes. Reserve it online, then try it in store. No obligation to buy.

    How are pianos delivered?

    A specialist piano team with lifting gear and padding. Free first tuning after two weeks.

    My child will outgrow it. Is a small size a waste?

    No: we buy back good used instruments at 50% when your child moves up a size.
