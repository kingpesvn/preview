Good to know

Do pre-owned consoles have a warranty?

Yes, 3 months for hardware faults. Every unit is cleaned, re-pasted and tested before sale.

Is the online trade-in value final?

It’s an estimate based on the condition you choose. We confirm after checking the console, usually at or near the estimate.

Do region versions matter?

Modern consoles are region-free. Retro systems may differ in voltage and video standard; we list it on each product.

Can you hold an item for pickup?

Yes. Order with “Pick up in store” and we hold it until the end of your pickup day.
