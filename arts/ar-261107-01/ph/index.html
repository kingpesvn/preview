    Quick rules

    New to the game? 1-minute read

    The basics of each game so you can step up and play. Staff are always happy to help.

    8-ball pool

    • One player takes solids (1–7), the other stripes (9–15)
    • Clear your group before shooting the 8
    • Call the pocket on the 8-ball
    • Scratching the cue ball is a foul: ball in hand for your opponent

    9-ball pool

    • Always hit the lowest-numbered ball first
    • Any ball may drop as long as the first contact was legal
    • Pocket the 9 legally to win the rack
    • Three fouls in a row lose the rack

    Three-cushion

    • Pocketless table; each player has their own cue ball
    • Your cue ball must hit at least three cushions before the second ball
    • Each success scores 1 and you keep shooting
    • Games usually go to 15, 25 or 40 points

    Snooker

    • Pot a red (1 point), then a colour (2–7), alternating
    • Colours are re-spotted while reds remain
    • With reds gone, pot colours in order yellow to black
    • Fouls give your opponent at least 4 points

    Good to know

    How is play charged?

    By the minute from when the table light goes on until it goes off; tables and rooms carry a peak surcharge after 5 pm.

    Do I need to book?

    Weekdays usually have tables; Friday and Saturday nights are best booked ahead.

    What do members get?

    15% (monthly) or 25% (annual VIP) off tables and rooms, plus priority bookings at peak time.

    Can kids play?

    Yes with an adult and before 9 pm; the table area is smoke-free.
