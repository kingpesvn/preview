      Dive sites

      Where we dive

      Our regular sites, their depth and what you might see. Tap a site.

      Chumphon Pinnacle

      • Depth 12–36 m (40–120 ft) · Advanced
      • Granite pinnacle covered in anemones
      • Schooling barracuda, batfish, whale sharks in season
      • Current can be strong: we dive it on calm mornings

      Sail Rock

      • Depth 6–40 m (20–130 ft) · Advanced
      • Vertical chimney swim-through from 18 m to 6 m
      • Big schools of trevally and chevron barracuda
      • Full-day trip with two dives

      Twins

      • Depth 5–18 m (15–60 ft) · Open Water
      • Two boulders with coral gardens around them
      • Clownfish, blue-spotted rays, nudibranchs
      • Our favourite site for courses

      Japanese Gardens

      • Depth 2–12 m (6–40 ft) · Snorkel and try dive
      • Shallow hard-coral garden in a sheltered bay
      • Turtles in the morning
      • Good for nervous first-timers

      Good to know

      Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

      For courses you swim 200 m without stopping (any style, no time limit) and float for 10 minutes. The try dive needs no swim test.

      How long after diving can I fly?

      Common guidance is at least 12 hours after a single dive and 18 hours after several dives or days of diving. Use the checker at the top of the page and ask your doctor if unsure.

      I am certified but have not dived in a year

      Book a refresher: a short pool session before your first fun dive. Tell us your last dive date in the booking form.

      What if the weather is bad?

      We move you to a sheltered site or another day at no cost. If we cancel, you get a full refund for that dive.

      Is equipment included?

      Full gear is included with every course and try dive. For fun dives, bring your own or rent per day.
