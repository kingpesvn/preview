    Feature

    Three Vietnamese, one winter under Mont Blanc

    In winter 2014, Khang took a group of Vietnamese travellers skiing for the first time. Nobody understood the instructor, and half the group gave up after one morning.

    The next season he qualified as an instructor and started a small class in Vietnamese. Groups of 8 at most, sorted by level, and the first session always in the snow garden.

    Today the school teaches in three languages, with private family lessons, kids’ groups and guided off-piste. One thing hasn’t changed: nobody gets left behind on the slope.

    On day one, the only goals are to stop and to smile.Lê Minh Khang, head of school

    Good to know

    I’ve never skied. Which lesson?

    The beginner group for 3–5 mornings, or a 2-hour private lesson to progress faster. Day one is always in the snow garden.

    Is the lift pass included?

    No. Passes are sold by the resort; beginner areas are often cheap or free. We only charge for lessons and rental.

    From what age can children learn?

    From 4 in the snow garden. 3-year-olds take a 1-hour private lesson with a parent.

    What if the weather is bad?

    If the resort closes the lifts, we reschedule or refund that session. Cancel 24 hours ahead for a full refund.
