    Good to know

    How long does a suit take?

    Usually 10–14 days with two try-ons. Wedding suits and gowns should be ordered 3–6 weeks ahead.

    I live far away. Can I still order?

    Yes. Send your measurements using our guide, or post a garment that fits you well. The final try-on can be done by video call.

    How much is the deposit?

    50% when you are measured and choose the fabric; the rest on collection.

    Can I bring my own fabric?

    Yes, with 20% off the making price. Bring it to the fitting so we can check width and shrinkage.

    What if it does not fit?

    We alter it free for 30 days after collection. If the mistake is ours, we remake it.
