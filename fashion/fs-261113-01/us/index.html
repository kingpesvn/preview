Good to know

Do I have to wash the costume?

No. Return it as it is; cleaning is part of the rental price. Please do not put it in a washing machine.

How is the deposit handled?

You pay it with the rental. We refund it within 3 days of the return, minus any repair beyond small tears.

What if my size is not listed?

Book a temporary alteration or message us with your height and chest. Many pieces have lacing or adjustable straps.

Can you ship?

Yes. We ship two days before your event and include a prepaid return label. Drop it off the day after.

Do you rent licensed character costumes?

We rent original and historical pieces. Many cosplayers combine them with their own accessories; we are happy to suggest pieces.
