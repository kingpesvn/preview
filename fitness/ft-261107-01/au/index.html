Good to know

I’ve never done Pilates. Can I join?

Yes. The trial helps us place you in a beginner class or a private session.

What’s the difference between mat and reformer?

Mat uses your body weight on the floor; the reformer uses springs to assist or resist, which makes alignment easier to coach.

Can I train while pregnant?

We have pre and postnatal sessions, but please get your doctor’s approval first.

What if my package expires?

Each package shows its validity; you can extend once by 14 days if you tell us ahead.
