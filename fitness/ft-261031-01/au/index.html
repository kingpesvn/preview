02 Levels

From first splash to water safe

Swimmers move up when they can do the skills, not after a set number of lessons. Parents get a progress note at the end of each course.

  1. 1
    Getting comfortableLevel 1

    Enter the pool confidently, blow bubbles, put the face in, stand up safely.

  2. 2
    Float and breatheLevel 2

    Front and back floats, glide with a kickboard, breathe out underwater in rhythm.

  3. 3
    Swim 10 metresLevel 3

    Kick and arms together, 10 m of breaststroke or freestyle without a float.

  4. 4
    Swim 25 metresLevel 4

    A full length of the pool, tread water for 30 seconds, roll onto the back to rest.

  5. 5
    Water safeLevel 5

    Swim 50 m, handle falling in wearing clothes, know how to call for help without jumping in to rescue.

What to bring

  • Swimsuit and swim cap
  • Goggles (sold at the front desk)
  • Towel and pool sandals
  • Water bottle
  • Under-5s: swim nappy

04 Pool & safety

Our pool and safety

An indoor pool open all year, with family changing rooms.

  • 25 m · 6 lanesIndoor pool
  • 0.6–0.9 m deepKids pool
  • 29–31 °CWater temperature
  • Every lessonLifeguard on duty
  • 3 times a dayWater tested
  • Up to 6 kids per coachClass size

Safety rules

  • A lifeguard on the deck during every lesson
  • Under-6s need an adult waiting at the pool
  • A 5-minute warm-up before each lesson
  • No running on the deck, no deep diving without a coach
  • Stay home if unwell, feverish or with ear pain, and book a make-up

Good to know

How many lessons until my child can swim?

Every child is different. Most 6–12 year olds swim 10 m after a 12-lesson course; younger or nervous children may need longer. Coaches send a note after each course.

Can parents watch?

Yes, there is seating overlooking the pool. In the 3–5 class a parent can join in the water.

Can we make up a missed lesson?

Up to two make-ups per course with 4 hours’ notice, in a class of the same level with space.

Can adults who are afraid of water learn?

Yes. The fear-of-water class has three people at most, starts in the shallows and never rushes you. You can try one lesson first.

Is the trial lesson free?

The Saturday morning trial is free; just book ahead so a coach can be ready.
