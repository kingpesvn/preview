Good to know

How early should I order?

Enter your wedding date in the filter: each piece shows the last day to order. Made-to-order veils take 7–14 days to sew, plus shipping, and we aim to deliver a week before the wedding.

Which veil length suits my dress?

Use the veil guide: dress shape and venue suggest a length, and your height shows roughly where it ends. Long veils suit churches and ball gowns; short veils suit beaches and city halls.

Ivory or diamond white?

Match the dress, not the photos. Send us a photo of your dress tag or fabric in daylight and we will tell you which net is closer.

Do you ship to Korea?

Yes. Standard takes about 10 days and express about 4 to Seoul and Busan. Import duties, if any, are paid on delivery.

Can I return a veil?

In-stock tulle veils can be returned within 30 days if unworn. Made-to-order veils are cut to your length, so they are final sale.
