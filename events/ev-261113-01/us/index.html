    Good to know

    How is your fee calculated?

    Full planning and Destination Italy are a percentage of your total budget with a minimum fee; partial planning and wedding-month coordination are flat fees. The budget planner on this page shows the fee for your numbers.

    Do vendors pay you commissions?

    No. We are paid only by you, so we recommend vendors for fit and value, not kickbacks.

    Is our date too soon?

    Enter it in the countdown above. Full planning needs about 9 months, partial planning 5 and coordination just one; we will tell you honestly what is still possible.

    Can we legally marry in Italy?

    Yes, with a civil or religious ceremony, or a symbolic one after signing at home. We prepare the documents and book the translator; allow 3 to 4 months for paperwork.

    How many weddings do you take on one date?

    One. Your lead planner is with you from the first call to the last dance.
