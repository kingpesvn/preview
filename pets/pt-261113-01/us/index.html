How we train

Kind, clear and step by step

  1. 01

    Meet and assess

    A short call, then we watch your dog for ten minutes before choosing a class.

  2. 02

    Teach it easy

    New skills start in a quiet room with good food, so your dog wins early and often.

  3. 03

    Add real life

    We add distance, time and distractions one at a time: other dogs, doors, the street.

  4. 04

    Take it home

    You leave each week with a ten-minute plan and can message us when you get stuck.

Good to know

What training methods do you use?

Reward-based training: we mark and pay the behaviour we want with food, toys and play, and we set the dog up so mistakes are rare. We do not use prong, choke or shock collars.

What if I miss a class?

Tell us the day before and you get the handout plus one make-up visit in another group that month.

Which vaccinations does my dog need?

Adult dogs: rabies and distemper/parvo, up to date. Puppies: at least two rounds of distemper/parvo. Bring the vet record to the first class.

My dog barks and lunges at other dogs. Can we join a class?

Start with private sessions. A group is too much at first; most dogs can move into a class after four to six sessions.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest trainer can. Results depend on practice at home; we promise a clear plan, small steps and support between sessions.
