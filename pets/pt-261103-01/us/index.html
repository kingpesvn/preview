02 A day here

Your pet’s day

A steady routine keeps meals and naps on time, just like home.

  1. 07:00
    Morning walk

    A 20-minute potty break and stroll on the lawn.

  2. 08:00
    Breakfast

    Exactly the food and amount you left with us.

  3. 10:00
    Group play

    Play with dogs of a similar size and temperament. Shy pets play one-to-one.

  4. 12:00
    Nap time

    Lights dimmed and soft music on.

  5. 16:30
    Walk and dinner

    We send you a photo update.

  6. 21:00
    Last call before bed

    Night staff check every room twice.

What to bring

  • Their usual food, portioned by meal
  • Vaccination record
  • Any medication and how to give it
  • Something that smells of home: an old T-shirt or blanket

Good to know

Can I watch my pet on camera?

Yes. At check-in we give you a login to watch your pet’s room on your phone, day and night.

What if my pet is a picky eater?

Bring their usual food. We feed on schedule and log how much they eat at each meal.

What if my pet gets sick during the stay?

We call you right away and take your pet to our partner vet five minutes away. Vet fees are paid by the owner.

Can I pick up earlier or later than planned?

Yes, with a day’s notice. Pick-up after noon adds half a night; after 6 pm adds a full night.
