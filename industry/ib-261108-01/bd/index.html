Process

From sample to carton

  1. 1Brief

    Send a sample, design file or idea, the quantity and your date.

  2. 2Sample

    A real sample in 5 working days to approve fabric, fit and print.

  3. 3Approve and deposit

    Sign, then lock sizes, colours and ship date.

  4. 4Bulk production

    Marker cutting and line sewing, with progress updates by message.

  5. 5Inspection

    Every piece checked against the approved sample; photos and defect count sent before packing.

  6. 6Pack and ship

    Folded, bagged and cartoned by size; delivered to your warehouse or forwarder.

  7. Inspection photos before shipping

Good to know

What is the minimum order?

Usually 100 pieces per style (totes 200, uniform shirts 50). Smaller runs are possible with a surcharge shown in the calculator.

Do you charge for samples?

There is a sample fee, credited back when you place the bulk order.

I only have an idea, no design yet.

Our pattern makers suggest fabric and draft the style; send reference pictures and we make a sample for you to approve.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, via courier or your forwarder; page prices exclude shipping and duties.
