02

Not sure what’s wrong?

Pick what you’re seeing for the likely repair, plus a quick tip to try first.

  • The screen is cracked or black

    If the phone still rings or vibrates, the screen is usually the only problem.

    Usually: Screen replacement

  • The battery dies fast or shuts off

    Batteries wear out after 2–3 years. A swollen battery lifts the screen: stop using it.

    Usually: Battery replacement

  • It won’t charge

    Try another cable first. Pocket lint in the port is the most common cause.

    Usually: Charging port repair · Battery replacement

  • It got wet

    Turn it off and don’t charge it. Rice doesn’t help; bring it in the same day.

    Usually: Water damage treatment

  • Photos are blurry

    Check the lens for cracks and remove the case: some cases block the flash.

    Usually: Camera repair

  • It won’t turn on

    Charge it for 30 minutes with a known-good cable. If nothing, we diagnose it free.

    Usually: Battery replacement · Board-level repair

  • The back glass is shattered

    Cover it with tape until the repair so glass doesn’t cut your hand.

    Usually: Back glass repair

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are welcome. Booking a time means your part is ready and you skip the line.

Will I lose my photos and data?

Screen, battery and port repairs don’t touch your data. Still, back up first if you can; board repairs carry a small risk and we tell you before we start.

What is the difference between standard and premium parts?

Standard parts are quality aftermarket parts. Premium parts are original-grade, with the closest color, brightness and touch feel. Both carry our warranty.

Does the warranty cover new drops?

The warranty covers parts and our workmanship, not new cracks or liquid damage. A new drop is a new repair.

Can you fix water damage?

Often, but not always. Turn it off, don’t charge it and bring it in the same day. If we can’t save it, you only pay the treatment.
