      Good to know

      I don’t know much about parts. How do I choose?

      Tap a use under “What will you use it for?” and a sensible build loads in. Swap any part you like, or message us for advice.

      How is the power supply worked out?

      We add up the CPU, graphics card and about 75 W for everything else, then add 25% headroom. Power supplies below that are locked so the PC stays stable.

      Do you offer instalments and invoices?

      Yes. Add a note with your order and we’ll call back with instalment options and take your invoice details.

      How does the warranty work?

      Parts carry the maker’s warranty and store builds get 36 months. Faults in the first 15 days get a new part.
