    Good to know

    Do you ship abroad?

    Yes, by international post with tracking, 7–14 days depending on the country. We quote the fee before sending.

    Can I take lacquer and pottery on a plane?

    Yes. We pack them in protective wrap for free and can box them to fit hand luggage.

    How long do the treats keep?

    Dried fruit, tamarind and mango 6 months; coffee 12 months. The date is printed on each pack.

    Is there a group discount?

    10% off from 20 items. We pack a bag per person and deliver to your hotel.
