Hands have stopped

A quartz battery usually lasts 2–3 years. If it stops right after a new battery, the movement may need a service.

  • Watch battery replacementfrom $12.00 · 15 minutes, while you wait
  • Mechanical watch servicefrom $120.00 · 10–14 days

Running fast or slow

A mechanical watch drifting minutes a day usually has dried oil or magnetism. We demagnetise free during the check.

  • Mechanical watch servicefrom $120.00 · 10–14 days
  • Pocket and vintage watch repairfrom $25.00 · 2–4 weeks

Fog under the crystal

Moisture inside can rust the movement. Bring it in soon to open, dry and re-gasket.

  • Water resistance testfrom $20.00 · 1 day
  • Mechanical watch servicefrom $120.00 · 10–14 days

Cracked crystal or scratched case

Replace a cracked crystal straight away to keep dust out. Case scratches can be polished.

  • Watch crystal replacementfrom $30.00 · 1 day
  • Case polishingfrom $60.00 · 2–3 days

Worn or loose strap

Change a worn leather strap before it snaps. A loose bracelet just needs a link removed.

  • Leather watch strapfrom $35.00 · 15 minutes, while you wait
  • Metal bracelet sizingfrom $10.00 · 15 minutes, while you wait

Sole coming off

If the sole is still good and only lifting, we re-glue it. If it is worn through, replace it.

  • Sole reattachmentfrom $15.00 · 1 day
  • Shoe resolingfrom $55.00 · 5–7 days

Worn, noisy heels

Replace heel tips early so the heel block is not damaged. Done in 30 minutes.

  • Heel repairfrom $15.00 · 30 minutes

Dirty, stained or smelly

Canvas and leather sneakers are hand-washed; suede needs a dry clean and maybe a re-dye.

  • Sneaker cleaningfrom $20.00 · 2–3 days
  • Suede cleaning and re-dyefrom $25.00 · 2–3 days
  • Leather shoe shine and restorationfrom $12.00 · 1 day

Shoes too tight

Leather shoes can be eased about half a size at the toes or sides. Synthetic and canvas stretch little.

  • Shoe stretchingfrom $20.00 · 1–2 days

Boot zip stuck or broken

A stuck zip sometimes only needs a new slider; broken teeth mean a new zip.

  • Boot zip replacementfrom $30.00 · 5–7 days

Split seam or worn handle

We hand re-stitch the seam and reinforce the handle for years more use.

  • Leather bag and wallet stitchingfrom $25.00 · 5–7 days

Faded leather, worn corners

We match the original colour and add a protective coat against dirt.

  • Leather colour restorationfrom $70.00 · 10–14 days

Broken bag zip

A broken puller is a same-day fix; a zip that splits needs a full replacement.

  • Bag zip replacementfrom $12.00 · 2–3 days

Good to know

Are the website prices final?

They are guide prices. We inspect the item and confirm the final price; we only start once you agree.

How long do repairs take?

Batteries and heels while you wait. Resoling 5–7 days; a watch service 10–14 days.

Do you collect items?

Yes, within the city. A small pickup fee applies, free on larger tickets.

Do you repair luxury watches?

We inspect all watches. If yours is still under the maker’s warranty, take it to the brand to keep that warranty.

How does the warranty work?

Each service has its own term, written on your slip. Our faults within that term are fixed free.
