    Good to know

    How is the power cost worked out?

    Average power × hours a day × 30 days × a reference electricity rate. Real tariffs vary, so use it to compare models.

    What does air-con installation include?

    Labour plus 3 m of copper pipe, cable and bracket. Longer runs or thick concrete walls are quoted before work starts.

    What do I need for a monthly plan?

    A credit card with enough limit, or ID and documents required by the finance partner. Approval depends on your application.

    What if the appliance is faulty?

    Within 7 days, manufacturing defects get a new unit. After that, in-home warranty per the maker’s policy.
