Good to know

How do I know if a sofa will fit through my door?

Use "Will it fit?" above or on each product page: enter your door width and height. A piece passes if its smallest side, tilted on end, is narrower than the door. Tight stairwells: send us a photo and we will check.

What does "made to order" mean?

The piece is built in your fabric or size after you order, usually in 4–8 weeks. In-stock pieces ship in 3–5 days.

How does the room set discount work?

Buy any three or more pieces from the same room set and we take 10% off those pieces. Mention the set in your order note.

Can I return furniture?

Yes, within 30 days if unused and in original packaging. Made-to-order fabrics are final sale, so order free samples first.

Do you assemble the furniture?

Add assembly for $49 per piece. Room-of-choice delivery includes unpacking and taking the boxes away.
