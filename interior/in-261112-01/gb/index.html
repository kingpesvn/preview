    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a garden design cost?

    A site consultation is $250 and is credited if you go ahead. Master plans start at $1,800 for small yards. Use the budget planner above for a rough build cost.

    When should I book?

    Spring installs fill up by January. Booking a design in winter means we can build as soon as the soil is ready.

    Do you only design, or also build?

    Both. You can take the plan to any contractor, but most clients have our own crew build it.

    What if a plant dies?

    Plants we install have a one-year warranty when they are watered as planned. We replace them for free.

    Are prices final?

    Prices here are a guide. You get a fixed written quote after the site visit, before any work starts.
