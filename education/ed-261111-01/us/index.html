A day at school

Your child’s day

A gentle rhythm of learning and play, with a proper nap.

  1. 06:45
    Arrival and free play
  2. 07:30
    Breakfast
  3. 08:00
    Morning exercise
  4. 08:30
    Learning time

    Stories, number games, art and music around the weekly theme.

  5. 09:45
    Outdoor play

    Sandpit, slides, swings and the vegetable patch.

  6. 10:30
    Lunch
  7. 11:30
    Nap time

    A separate nap room, with a teacher on watch throughout.

  8. 14:00
    Wash up and snack
  9. 14:45
    Activity corners and clubs

    Building, books, dance and English, depending on the class.

  10. 16:00
    Pick-up

    We look after children until 5:30 pm for late pick-ups.

Good to know

From what age do you take children?

We take children from age 2 (by birth year). Enter a date of birth in the Classes section to see the right class.

Can children join mid-year?

Yes, if there are places. The first month is charged by days attended.

What about food allergies?

Tell us at enrolment; the kitchen cooks an alternative and the tray is labelled.

Do you offer transport?

Within 5 km, charged monthly. Ask about routes when you visit.
