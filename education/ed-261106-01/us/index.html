Good to know

Is the trial lesson free?

Yes. The first lesson is free; fees start from the second lesson if you continue.

How do I pay?

Monthly or per 8-lesson pack, by transfer or cash at the centre.

What if my child misses a lesson?

With 12 hours’ notice we schedule a make-up within the month; late cancellations count as taught.

Can we change tutors?

Yes, free within 48 hours if it isn’t a good fit, no questions asked.
