03 A day here

A day on the farm

A suggested plan for the full day. Come in the morning for berries and feeding.

  1. 08:00
    Welcome drink

    Pick up a map and a feed basket and hear the 5-minute farm rules.

  2. 08:30
    Feeding time

    Calves, goats, rabbits and hens are waiting for breakfast.

  3. 09:30
    Strawberry picking

    Berries taste sweetest in the cool morning.

  4. 11:30
    Farm lunch

    Dishes made with the farm’s own vegetables and eggs.

  5. 13:30
    Baking class

    Strawberry tarts or pumpkin bread to take home.

  6. 15:30
    Tea hill walk and farm shop

    Watch the sunset and pick up vegetables and jams.

What to bring

  • Walking shoes, boots if it rains
  • Hat and sunscreen
  • A light jacket for chilly mornings
  • A tote bag for produce

Good to know

Do toddlers need a ticket?

Children under 3 come free; just add them in the form so we can plan.

What if it rains?

The animal barn, baking class and eating area are covered. In heavy rain you can move to another day.

Can we bring our own food?

Yes, there is a picnic area. Please don’t feed the animals food from outside.

Are you open on Mondays?

We close on Mondays to tend the gardens, except public holidays. Other days we open 7:30 am to 5 pm.
