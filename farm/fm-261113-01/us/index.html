Keep it fresh

How to store fish at home

  • Keep it very cold

    Store fish at 32–38°F in the coldest part of the fridge, on a plate over ice if you can.

  • Eat it soon

    Each card shows "best within" days. Oily fish like mackerel is a same-day fish.

  • Freeze it right

    Pat dry, wrap tight with no air and freeze flat. Use within three months.

  • Let shellfish breathe

    Live mussels, clams and oysters need air: a bowl covered with a damp towel, never a sealed bag or fresh water.

Good to know

How fresh is the fish?

Local fish is bought from day boats the same morning. The "Today's board" at the top lists what came in today; if a species is not on it, ask us before you order.

Why is the final price a little different from the estimate?

Fish is sold by weight and no two fish are the same. We cut as close as we can to what you ask for and charge for the actual weight.

What does "sashimi-grade" mean here?

Fish we would eat raw ourselves: handled cold from the boat and, where needed, frozen to the temperature and time food-safety rules require. Everything else should be cooked.

Do you ship?

Yes, overnight in an insulated box with gel ice, Tuesday to Friday. Order by 2 pm for delivery the next day.

Can you clean and cut the fish for me?

Scaling, gutting, filleting and pin-boning are free. Tell us in the notes how you plan to cook it.
