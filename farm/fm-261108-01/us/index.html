Good to know

Do florists get weekly pricing?

Yes. Orders from 20 bunches get the volume discount; weekly buyers receive a price list by message every Monday.

Can I order flowers out of season?

We only cut what is growing. If your date falls outside a flower’s season, we suggest the closest substitute flower or colour.

Will flowers wilt on long trips?

Flowers are cut early, conditioned and packed cold, and travel by overnight cold truck. Put them in water on arrival and change it daily.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes, on Saturday and Sunday mornings; please message us first.
