01 Where it hurts

Common problems we treat

Pick what you feel to see matching problems, or filter by area. Every card shows the usual number of visits.

What do you feel?

Desk stiffness usually starts in the neck and upper back. A few adjustments plus hourly breaks and a raised screen help most people within weeks.

Pain running below the knee can come from an irritated nerve in the low back. We test nerve function on the first visit before choosing treatment.

Sudden low back pain after lifting is common and usually settles. Keep walking gently; avoid bed rest.

Tension headaches often come from tight neck and shoulder muscles. If a headache is sudden and severe, see a doctor first.

Training pain often comes from how the hip, knee and shoulder move together. We look at the whole chain, not just the sore spot.

Low back and pelvic pain are common in the second and third trimester. We use side-lying and pillow techniques only.

Go to a doctor or the ER first if you have

  • Numbness in the groin, or loss of bladder or bowel control: go to the emergency room now
  • Sudden weakness in an arm or leg
  • Back pain with fever, or weight loss you cannot explain
  • A fall, car crash or hard hit in the last few days
  • The worst headache of your life, or a headache with a stiff neck and fever

04 Home exercises

Keep the progress going

Simple steps between visits. If anything gets worse, call us.

  1. First 24 hours

    Mild soreness is normal. Walk gently, drink water, and use heat or ice for 15 minutes if it helps.

  2. At your desk

    Stand up every 45 minutes; roll the shoulders back and tuck the chin for 10 seconds.

  3. Days 2–7

    Do your two home exercises once a day. Stop any that make pain spread down a limb.

  4. Week 2 onward

    Add a 20-minute walk most days. Sleep on your side with a pillow between the knees.

  5. If it gets worse

    Call us the same day. Go to the ER for any of the warning signs listed above.

Good to know

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If your insurance needs a referral for reimbursement, ask your doctor and bring it to the first visit.

Does an adjustment hurt?

Most people feel pressure and sometimes a pop, not pain. We use gentler techniques if you prefer, and we never adjust without your OK.

How many visits will I need?

Each condition card shows a usual range. We give you a plan after the first exam and recheck every few visits; if you are not improving, we refer you on.

Do you take X-rays?

Only when the exam suggests a need, such as after a fall. We refer you to imaging nearby rather than X-ray everyone.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, like leggings or sweatpants and a T-shirt.
