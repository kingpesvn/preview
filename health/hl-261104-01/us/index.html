Good to know

Do I need a doctor’s referral?

Not for common muscle and joint pain. After surgery or a stroke, please bring your discharge notes.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends; your physio estimates after the assessment. Many muscle and joint cases need around 6–10 sessions plus home exercise.

Does it hurt?

You may feel tired or stretched, but not sharp pain. Tell us your 0–10 pain level and we adjust.

Does insurance cover it?

We issue invoices and treatment notes for private health insurance. Cover depends on your policy.
