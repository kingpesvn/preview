Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

For weddings, 2–3 months ahead, especially in the busy year-end season. Free dates are shown in the calendar.

How does the deposit work?

A 30% deposit holds the date; the rest is paid after the event. Cancel more than 30 days ahead for a refund.

Do you take song requests?

Yes. Send a must-play and do-not-play list before the event; requests on the night are welcome if they fit the mood.

What if the venue has its own sound?

Choose the smallest sound option; I bring the decks and check the house system before guests arrive.
